How it’ll go.

Four steps over three weeks. No case-interview theatrics.

Week 1

Info sessions

Two open sessions plus a coffee-chat window. Come with questions; nothing is evaluated here.

Week 2

Application due

A short form, a résumé, and one written response. Fifteen minutes if you don't overthink it.

Week 3

Conversation

A 25-minute chat with two board members about a problem you found interesting. No brain teasers.

Week 3

Decisions & onboarding

Offers go out, teams get staffed, and training starts the same week.

Dates
Exact dates lock in once we're through the student-group recognition process. Apply anyway — we'll email everyone in the pool as soon as the calendar is real.
What actually matters

We are not
screening for
a résumé.

We expect a good chunk of the founding cohort to have never written a line of SQL. That's teachable. What isn't is the instinct to ask why a number looks the way it does.

You notice when something is off

A total that doesn't add up, an average hiding two populations, a survey nobody filled out. That reflex is the whole job.

You can talk to a business owner

Most of the people we want to help have never used the word "dataset." Explaining a finding plainly matters as much as producing it.

You finish the boring part

Cleaning is 60% of the work and it is nobody's favorite. Teams run on people who do it anyway.

You're fine building from nothing

There's no playbook here yet. The first cohort writes it, which is either the appeal or the dealbreaker.

 The deal

            
What you’d get.

          

        

        

          

            
🧹

            
Your data, actually usable

            
Whatever you've got — POS exports, spreadsheets, a CRM nobody maintains — consolidated, cleaned, documented, and handed back in a form you can keep querying.

          

          

            
🎯

            
One question, answered

            
Not a survey of everything. One decision you're trying to make, worked properly, with an honest statement of how confident we are.

          

          

            
📊

            
A dashboard you keep

            
A live view of the numbers that matter to you, plus documentation and a walkthrough so it survives after the semester ends.

          

          

            
🗣️

            
Plain language

            
A final presentation with no jargon, and a written recommendation you could hand to a manager without translating it first.

          

          

            
🔒

            
An NDA if you want one

            
Your data stays yours. We'll sign a confidentiality agreement, anonymize anything that appears publicly, and delete what you ask us to.

          

          

            
🧾

            
No invoice

            
Free for our first cohort of clients, and permanently free for nonprofits. We're buying experience; you're buying nothing.

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          

            
 Your side

            
What we’d
need from you.

            

              Not much, but the parts we do need are non-negotiable. Engagements fail when the client
              can't get us data or can't tell us what decision they're trying to make.
            

          

          

            

              
A real question

              
Something you'd act on if you knew the answer. "Should I keep the Sunday shift?" beats "tell me about my business."

            

            

              
Access to whatever data exists

              
Messy is fine. Incomplete is fine. Four different spreadsheets with conflicting totals is genuinely fine — that's the work.

            

            

              
About two hours a month

              
A kickoff conversation, a check-in every two weeks, and a final presentation. That's the whole time commitment.

            

            

              
One named contact

              
Someone who can answer "what does this column mean?" without a three-week delay. Usually the owner or an ops lead.

            

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          

            
 The twelve weeks

            
How an engagement runs.

          

        

        

          

            
Weeks 1–2

            
Scoping & audit

            
We sit with you, pin down the decision, and inventory what data actually exists. You can walk away here at no cost.

          

          

            
Weeks 3–7

            
Build

            
Cleaning, pipeline, analysis. Checkpoint with you every two weeks so nothing drifts off-target.

          

          

            
Weeks 8–11

            
Analyze & test

            
The actual answer, plus a sanity check against anything you already believe to be true.

          

          

            
Week 12

            
Hand off

            
Presentation, dashboard, documentation, and the cleaned data — yours to keep either way.
