How it runs

What being on this board means.

🧭

You set the precedent

There is no "how we've always done it." The first board writes the scoping template, the QA bar, and the client agreement — and then lives with them.

🤝

You carry a client

Every board member sits on at least one engagement. This isn't an oversight role; it's a working one.

📣

You recruit

The founding board is also the founding sales and recruitment team. Getting the first client and the first cohort is the job in year one.
